3. Main findings
The main P5 benchmark is the CCC Seventh Carbon Budget Balanced Pathway. The primary DESNZ comparison basis is the including-IAS emissions series, because it is closer to the CCC economy-wide total pathway. The CCC Sixth Carbon Budget Balanced Net Zero Pathway is retained only as a sensitivity check.

Key results:
- In 2030, the DESNZ including-IAS baseline is 72.2 MtCO2e above the CCC7 Balanced Pathway.
- By 2035, the gap widens to 154.8 MtCO2e.
- By 2050, the gap reaches 325.4 MtCO2e.
- Against the CCC6 Balanced Net Zero Pathway, the 2050 sensitivity gap is 325.3 MtCO2e, almost identical to the CCC7 result.
- Using the narrower DESNZ excluding-IAS baseline, the 2050 gap against CCC7 is still 291.4 MtCO2e, showing that a large residual gap remains even under a narrower accounting scope.
- The cumulative 2025-2050 DESNZ including-IAS gap against CCC7 is 4550.7 MtCO2e. This is useful as a supplementary result, but should not replace the main annual headline metrics.

4. Link to CB6
Over the CB6 period (2033-2037), the CCC7 Balanced Pathway sums to 944.2 MtCO2e, which is 20.8 MtCO2e below the statutory CB6 target of 965.0 MtCO2e. This indicates that the CCC7 pathway is broadly consistent with the CB6 level.

By contrast, DESNZ official comparison emissions over CB6 are 1702.5 MtCO2e, producing an official CB6 gap of 737.5 MtCO2e. Therefore, the P5 annual benchmark gap and the P4 official CB6 delivery gap are two views of the same policy-delivery problem: one measured against a target-consistent annual pathway, and the other measured over a statutory carbon-budget period.

5. Figure interpretation
The pathway figure shows that the DESNZ EEP 2024 baseline flattens after 2030 and remains at around 324.3 MtCO2e including IAS in 2050. In contrast, the CCC7 Balanced Pathway continues to fall towards net zero, reaching -1.1 MtCO2e in 2050. This creates a widening divergence after 2030.

The annual gap figure confirms that the DESNZ including-IAS minus CCC7 gap grows quickly from 2030 onward and reaches 325.4 MtCO2e in 2050. This supports the dissertation's core claim that the current-policy baseline is substantially misaligned with a target-consistent benchmark.

6. Suggested Results wording

7. Limitations and caveats
The main P5 comparison should remain at the economy-wide annual-emissions level. Sector-level CCC data can be used as broad context, but should not be used for strict one-to-one sector attribution, because CCC sector categories and DESNZ TES sectors do not align perfectly. The main caveats concern engineered removals, land use, transport, and buildings/product-use categories.
