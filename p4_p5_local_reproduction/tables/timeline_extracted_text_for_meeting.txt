P8. 22–28 July: Uncertainty framework, scenario design and synthesis 
Main tasks 
• Develop the uncertainty framework based on the “trumpet/cone” of uncertainty over time. 
• Define key uncertainty drivers: UK policy delivery, international climate action, fuel prices, technology costs, demand 
reduction and electrification pace. 
• Build the 2× 2 scenario matrix: high/low UK policy delivery and high/low international climate action / technology-cost 
environment. 
• Decide how much can be quantified and how much should remain qualitative. 
• If feasible, develop simple sensitivity estimates around the 2050 emissions gap. 
• Draft Method subsection on uncertainty and scenarios. 
• Prepare Discussion outline, including near-miss outcomes, negative emissions/credits and policy delivery uncertainty. 
Expected outputs 
• Uncertainty framework diagram. 
• 2× 2 scenario matrix. 
• Initial scenario assumptions table. 
• Draft Method subsection on uncertainty and scenarios. 
• Discussion outline.
