P4 Accounting Basis Note

Purpose
This note explains the accounting basis used in the P4 DESNZ baseline and carbon-budget gap analysis. The purpose is to avoid mixing different emissions scopes when interpreting the DESNZ current-policy pathway, official carbon-budget performance, and the 2050 baseline.

Accounting choices

1. Historical and DESNZ annual pathway
For the continuous historical-projection pathway, the analysis uses territorial greenhouse gas emissions excluding international aviation and shipping (IAS). This is because the official final UK GHG emissions series for 1990-2023 is consistent with the historical segment of DESNZ EEP Annex A excluding-IAS totals. The local reproducibility check shows a maximum difference of only 0.0024 MtCO2e between UK final GHG Table 1.1 and DESNZ Annex A excluding-IAS history, which is negligible for this dissertation.

2. Official carbon-budget performance
For carbon-budget-period performance, the analysis uses DESNZ Web Table 2.1 and follows the official comparison basis reported there. CB4 and CB5 are compared on an excluding-IAS basis, while CB6 is compared on a Net Carbon Account / including-IAS basis. Therefore, the main P4 policy-delivery metric is the official CB6 projected gap of 737.5 MtCO2e over 2033-2037.

3. Accounting caveat
The CB6 excluding-IAS gap is also reported as a supporting accounting caveat. It is 546.6 MtCO2e, which shows that the projected CB6 shortfall remains large even on a narrower territorial excluding-IAS basis. However, this is not the official CB6 comparison basis and should not replace the official 737.5 MtCO2e headline metric.

4. Link to P5 CCC benchmark comparison
For the annual DESNZ versus CCC benchmark comparison in P5, the DESNZ including-IAS series is used as the main comparator because it is closer to the CCC economy-wide total pathway. The excluding-IAS series remains useful for the historical territorial trend, but the including-IAS series is more appropriate when comparing with CCC total emissions pathways.

Recommended wording for Methods
The analysis separates three accounting uses. First, territorial emissions excluding IAS are used for the continuous historical-projection pathway, because the official UK final GHG series for 1990-2023 is consistent with the historical segment of DESNZ EEP Annex A excluding-IAS totals. Second, official carbon-budget performance is assessed using DESNZ Web Table 2.1 and its reported comparison basis: CB4 and CB5 exclude IAS, while CB6 uses the Net Carbon Account / including-IAS basis. Third, the DESNZ including-IAS annual pathway is used for the main comparison with CCC economy-wide pathways in P5. This separation ensures that accounting-scope differences are treated transparently rather than interpreted as substantive emissions differences.

Recommended wording for Results
On the official DESNZ carbon-budget comparison basis, the UK is projected to remain within CB4 and CB5 but to exceed CB6 substantially. DESNZ projects official comparison emissions of 1,702.5 MtCO2e against a CB6 budget of 965.0 MtCO2e, implying a projected CB6 gap of 737.5 MtCO2e over 2033-2037. On a narrower territorial excluding-IAS basis, the CB6 gap is smaller, at 546.6 MtCO2e, but remains substantial. The accounting distinction therefore changes the size of the estimated gap but not the conclusion that current-policy projections fall well short of the sixth carbon budget.
