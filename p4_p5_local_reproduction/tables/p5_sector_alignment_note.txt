P5 sector alignment note: DESNZ TES versus CCC Seventh Carbon Budget Balanced Pathway

Purpose
This note records whether the DESNZ Energy and Emissions Projections (EEP) TES sectors can be compared with the CCC Seventh Carbon Budget Balanced Pathway sectors. It is a methodological bridge for P5, not a full sector-by-sector attribution of the 2050 gap.

Main conclusion
The economy-wide P5 comparison is robust, but sector-level alignment is only partly one-to-one. DESNZ TES sectors are suitable as the primary structure for identifying residual emissions in the DESNZ baseline. CCC sector data should be used as a benchmark/context layer, with direct sector comparisons limited to broad matches.

Direct or broadly usable matches
Agriculture, electricity supply, fuel supply, industry, and waste can be compared at a broad sector level. These comparisons should still carry a short caveat because the underlying source-category boundaries may not be identical.

Partial or cautious matches
Buildings and product uses, domestic transport, IAS, and LULUCF are not exact one-to-one matches. CCC separates residential buildings, non-residential buildings, F-gases, surface transport, aviation, shipping, land use, and engineered removals in ways that do not map cleanly onto every DESNZ TES category.

Critical caveat
CCC has a separate Engineered removals sector in 2050 (-35.8 MtCO2e). DESNZ TES has no direct equivalent. Therefore engineered removals should be reported separately in P5/P6 and should not be folded into DESNZ LULUCF or any other DESNZ sector unless a separate accounting bridge is explicitly built.

Recommended dissertation use
Use the P5 table to state that CCC is appropriate as an economy-wide benchmark and as a broad sectoral comparator, but not as a strict sector-by-sector decomposition framework. For the next analysis step, use DESNZ TES residual emissions as the main sectoral driver evidence, and use CCC sector values selectively to show where target-consistent residual emissions are much lower.

Suggested wording
"Sector-level comparisons between the DESNZ EEP baseline and the CCC Seventh Carbon Budget Balanced Pathway are used cautiously because the two datasets do not share a fully identical sector classification. Direct broad comparisons are possible for agriculture, electricity supply, fuel supply, industry and waste. Other categories require aggregation or qualification, particularly buildings/product use, transport, IAS and land use. The CCC's engineered removals category is reported separately because it has no direct equivalent in the DESNZ TES sector baseline."
